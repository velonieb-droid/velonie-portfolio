A More Thoughtful Aesthetic Experience

Stillwater Aesthetics is a fictional Scottsdale med spa concept built around informed choices, clear expectations, and a consultation-first experience. Visitors can explore treatment categories, learn what questions to ask, and review provider qualifications before deciding whether to proceed.

A clear path from consultation to care

  1. Share your aesthetic goals and the type of service you would like to learn more about.
  2. Discuss your medical history privately with the appropriate qualified provider, who can assess suitability and explain alternatives, risks, and recovery.
  3. Review the proposed plan, total cost, aftercare, and follow-up expectations before choosing whether to proceed.

Questions to Ask Before Your Consultation

Who will perform my treatment?

Ask for the name, professional license, relevant training, and experience of the person who will perform the treatment. Confirm the medical director or supervising clinician where required and verify credentials with the appropriate state licensing board.

Is a consultation required before treatment?

A consultation is an important step for understanding your goals, reviewing relevant health information privately, assessing suitability, and discussing options. Some procedures may require additional evaluation or a separate appointment.

What risks, side effects, and downtime should I expect?

These vary by procedure, device, product, skin type, and individual factors. Ask the provider to explain common and serious risks, recovery expectations, aftercare, and when to seek medical attention.

How much do treatments cost?

Pricing can depend on the treatment, area, amount of product, number of sessions, provider, and clinic. Request an itemized total estimate, including any consultation, follow-up, or maintenance costs, before agreeing to treatment.

Are results guaranteed or permanent?

No treatment should be presented as a guaranteed result. Outcomes vary between individuals, and some services require maintenance or repeat sessions. Ask to review representative, consented examples and discuss realistic expectations.
