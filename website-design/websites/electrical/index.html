Frequently Asked Questions

When should I consider an electrical panel upgrade?

A panel assessment may be appropriate when adding major equipment, planning a renovation, experiencing recurring breaker trips, or when the panel is visibly damaged or outdated. A qualified electrician should assess the system.

Can I install a Level 2 EV charger at home?

Many homes can support a Level 2 charger, but the existing electrical service, panel capacity, circuit route, equipment specifications, and local permitting requirements need to be assessed by a qualified electrician.

What should I do if I notice flickering lights or repeated breaker trips?

Do not open the electrical panel or attempt repairs. If there is smoke, burning odor, sparks, heat, or immediate danger, move away and contact emergency services or the appropriate utility. Otherwise, arrange a qualified electrical inspection.

Do electrical projects require permits?

Permit requirements depend on the work and local jurisdiction. A real contractor should explain which permits and inspections apply to the specific project and who is responsible for obtaining them.
