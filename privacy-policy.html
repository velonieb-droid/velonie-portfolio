1. Introduction and scope

Velonie Automation (velonieautomation.com) is operated by Velonie Baluarte. This policy covers two separate things:

  • Website visitor data: information handled when you browse this website, use the AI chat assistant, or book a call (section 2).
  • SEO reporting application data: Google Search Console information accessed by Velonie Weekly SEO Report, an internal reporting application that retrieves authorized Search Console data and generates weekly SEO reports (section 3).

The two are kept distinct. Visiting this website does not give the SEO reporting application access to your Google account, and the application does not use website visitor data.

2. Website visitors

2.1 Google Analytics 4

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use it. GA4 uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as the pages you view, the referring website, your approximate location, your device and browser type, and how you interact with pages.

We also record a small number of interactions as analytics events, for example clicks on booking, contact or service links (with the link label and page path), video plays, section tab views, use of the free tools, and whether a booking made through the scheduling tool completed. These events are set up not to include names, email addresses, booking identifiers, or anything you type into the free tools.

GA4 data is processed by Google and kept according to the retention setting of our Analytics property. You can learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

2.2 Velonie AI Assistant (website chatbot)

The chat assistant on this website is provided by Easy-Peasy.AI. When the chat widget loads on a page, it shares the page address (URL), the page title, and the referring page with the Easy-Peasy.AI chat service. The widget also stores its display settings in your browser's local storage.

If you send a message, your message is processed by Easy-Peasy.AI to generate a response. Depending on the service's settings, conversation history may be stored by Easy-Peasy.AI and made available to us so we can respond to inquiries and improve the assistant. Please do not share passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in the chat. Easy-Peasy.AI handles this information under its own privacy policy.

2.3 Cal.com booking

The booking section of the homepage uses Cal.com for appointment scheduling. The scheduling interface loads when you reach the booking section or select a booking link. Information you submit through it, such as your name, email address, and any notes, is processed by Cal.com to schedule the appointment and is made available to us so we can hold the meeting. Cal.com handles this information under its own privacy policy. If you prefer not to use Cal.com, you can request a call by email instead.

2.4 Email and free tools

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to reply. The free tools on this website run in your browser. The SEO page checker requests the page address you enter directly from your browser; that request is not routed through a server we operate.

3. Velonie Weekly SEO Report (Google Search Console data)

Velonie Weekly SEO Report is an internal reporting application operated by Velonie Automation. It retrieves Google Search Console information for properties its owner has authorized and generates weekly SEO reports. It is not a public service, and it does not collect information from website visitors.

3.1 Information accessed

For the authorized Search Console properties, the application may access:

  • Search queries
  • Search clicks
  • Search impressions
  • Average click-through rate (CTR)
  • Average search position
  • Landing pages and website URLs
  • Performance by country
  • Performance by device
  • Search performance by date
  • Sitemap processing information, if this feature is enabled in the final implementation

3.2 How the information is used

The information is used only to:

  • Generate weekly SEO reports
  • Compare reporting periods
  • Identify keyword opportunities
  • Monitor search visibility
  • Evaluate website performance
  • Identify SEO improvement opportunities
  • Deliver reports to the authorized email recipient

3.3 Google OAuth authorization

The application uses Google OAuth. The account owner signs in with Google and grants access through Google's consent screen. The application is designed to request read-only access to Search Console data, along with the permission needed to send the report by email. It does not receive or collect Google passwords. Access can be revoked at any time through Google Account Connections.

3.4 Data storage and retention

The application is designed to retrieve Search Console results when a report runs, process them in Google Apps Script, and deliver the report through Gmail. It is not designed to keep a separate database of Search Console data. Some information is still retained as part of how these Google services work:

  • Report emails: each report contains Search Console data and remains in the sending account's Sent folder and the recipient's mailbox until deleted.
  • Configuration: settings such as the Search Console property and the report recipient may be stored as Apps Script properties until changed or removed.
  • Execution records and logs: Google Apps Script keeps records of script runs, and any log or error messages the script writes, according to Google's retention periods.
  • Authorization: Google manages the OAuth authorization for the account that approved access until it is revoked.

If the application later saves report history or cached results (for example in Google Sheets), this policy will be updated to describe what is stored and for how long.

3.5 Data sharing

Search Console data is handled through Google services that provide authentication (Google OAuth), API access (Google Search Console API), script execution (Google Apps Script), and email delivery (Gmail). Reports are sent only to the authorized recipient. The application does not sell Search Console data, use it for advertising, or share it with other third parties.

3.6 Google API Services User Data Policy

Velonie Weekly SEO Report's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including:

  • Google-managed sign-in and OAuth authorization, so passwords are never handled by the application
  • HTTPS for this website and for requests to Google APIs
  • Access to the reporting application and its Google account restricted to its owner
  • Requesting read-only Search Console access rather than permission to change settings

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your choices and revoking access

  • Analytics: block cookies in your browser or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Chat and booking: both are optional. You can contact us by email instead.
  • Google account access: remove Velonie Weekly SEO Report at https://myaccount.google.com/connections. Revoking authorization prevents the application from accessing Google data in the future. It does not delete reports that were already delivered by email.
  • Questions or deletion requests: email us about information we hold, such as booking details or messages you sent us.

6. Children's privacy

This website is intended for businesses and professionals, and Velonie Weekly SEO Report is an internal business reporting utility. Neither is directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the chat or booking tools, please contact us.

7. Policy updates

We will update this policy when the website, the SEO reporting application, or our data practices change, including any change to the Google data the application accesses or how it is stored. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
