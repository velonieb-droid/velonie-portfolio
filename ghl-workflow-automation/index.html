FAQ

GoHighLevel workflow questions.

Can GHL automatically follow up with new leads?

Yes. It can send SMS and email, assign users, create tasks and opportunities, notify the team, and change follow-up based on replies, bookings, or other events.

How do you prevent contacts from receiving the wrong messages?

Use precise triggers, eligibility filters, status checks, goals, stop conditions, opt-out handling, and tests for duplicate or conflicting enrollment.

Can workflows update opportunity stages?

Yes. Workflows can create opportunities, update stages, assign owners, record changes, and start different actions from stage movement.

Can you automate missed calls and no-shows?

Yes. Missed-call text back, appointment confirmations, reminders, rescheduling, no-show follow-up, and internal notifications can be automated.

Do you document the workflows?

Yes. Delivery includes logic and dependency notes, naming conventions, test scenarios, operational guidance, and handover documentation.
