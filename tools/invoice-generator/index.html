    How to create an invoice

    1. Enter your business name and contact details, and add a logo and accent colour if you want your branding on it.
    2. Enter the client, an invoice number, the dates and the currency.
    3. Add a line for each service or product with a quantity and a unit price. Amounts and totals update as you type.
    4. Add a discount or tax if they apply, and any payment terms in the notes.
    5. Click Save as PDF / Print and choose Save as PDF, or download the invoice as a standalone HTML file.

    How the totals are calculated

    • Line amount = quantity × unit price.
    • Subtotal = the sum of all line amounts.
    • Discount is taken off the subtotal, as a percentage or a fixed amount.
    • Tax = tax rate × (subtotal − discount).
    • Total due = subtotal − discount + tax.

    Amounts are rounded to two decimal places at each step.

    Good to know

    • Nothing is saved. If you close or reload the page the invoice is gone, so download or print it first.
    • Your details, your client's details and your logo are processed on your device and are never uploaded.
    • This tool formats an invoice. It is not accounting software and it is not tax or legal advice. Invoice requirements, such as tax numbers and wording, differ by country, so check what applies to you.
