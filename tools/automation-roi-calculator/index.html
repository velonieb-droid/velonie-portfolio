    How to use the calculator
    
    
      
      
    1. Pick tasks from the suggestions, or type your own, and enter the hours each takes per week and how many people do it.
      2. 
      
    2. Check the suggested share that could be automated and adjust it, then enter what an hour of that work costs you.
      3. 
      
    3. If slow follow-up is costing you leads, fill in the missed leads section.
      4. 
      
    4. Enter what automating would cost to see the first-year return and the payback period.
      5. 
    
    

    
    How the numbers are worked out
    
    
      
      
    • Hours saved per year = hours per week × people × share automatable × working weeks, added up across tasks.
      • 
      
    • Value of saved time = hours saved × cost per hour.
      • 
      
    • Recovered revenue = missed leads per month × share recoverable × close rate × average sale × 12.
      • 
      
    • Yearly benefit = value of saved time + recovered revenue.
      • 
      
    • First-year cost = setup cost + monthly cost × 12.
      • 
      
    • First-year ROI = (yearly benefit − first-year cost) ÷ first-year cost.
      • 
      
    • Payback period = setup cost ÷ (monthly benefit − monthly cost).
      • 
    
    

    
    Where the suggested percentages come from
    
    
    The suggestions are starting estimates based on how rule-based a task is. They are not measured statistics, and no published study gives a reliable figure for each of these tasks. Change them whenever your process is different.
    
    
      
      
    • About 90%: rule-based from start to finish, such as appointment reminders, review requests and copying data between tools. A trigger starts fixed steps.
      • 
      
    • About 80%: rule-based, with a person handling the exceptions, such as lead follow-up and booking changes.
      • 
      
    • About 70%: mostly rule-based, but someone reviews or approves the result, such as invoices and reports.
      • 
      
    • About 60%: the routine parts automate while the writing or decisions stay with a person, such as proposals and social posts.
      • 
      
    • About 50%: automation handles the common cases only, such as answering frequent customer questions.
      • 
    
    

    
    Read the result with care
    
    
      
      
    • This is arithmetic on the numbers you enter. It is an estimate, not a forecast or a guarantee.
      • 
      
    • Saved hours only become saved money if that time is used for other valuable work or the cost is actually removed.
      • 
      
    • Be conservative with the share that can be automated and the share of leads you could recover. Small changes there move the result a lot.
      • 
      
    • Costs such as maintenance, training and the time to manage a new system are not included unless you add them to the cost fields.
