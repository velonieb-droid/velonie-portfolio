      How to use the calculator

      1. Paste your text message. The counter shows the length, the number of segments and how much room is left.
      2. Check the notes for characters that push the message into the shorter format, and replace them with one click where a plain version exists.
      3. Enter how many people you are sending to and your price per segment to estimate the cost of the send.

      How SMS segments work

      A text message is sent in pieces called segments, and providers bill per segment, not per message.

      • Standard text (GSM-7): one segment holds 160 characters. A longer message is split into segments of 153, because each piece carries a small header so the phone can join them back together.
      • Special characters (UCS-2): if the message contains even one character outside the standard set, such as an emoji, a curly quote or a non-Latin letter, the whole message switches format. One segment then holds 70 characters, or 67 each when split.
      • Double-counted characters: in standard text, ^ { } [ ] \ ~ | and € each use two character spaces.

      Example

      A 150-character message in plain text is 1 segment. Add a single emoji and it becomes UCS-2, where 150 characters need 3 segments. For a send to 2,000 people, that is 6,000 billed segments instead of 2,000.

      Good to know

      • Curly quotes, long dashes and the single-character ellipsis often arrive unnoticed when text is pasted from a document or typed on a phone.
      • Merge fields such as {{contact.first_name}} are counted as typed. The real length depends on each contact, so leave some room.
      • The cost is an estimate from the rate you enter. Your provider's billing log is the final word, and some carriers handle encoding differently.
      • Your message is processed on your device and is not uploaded or stored.
