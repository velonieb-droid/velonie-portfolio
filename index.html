How I work

Four steps. Every project, every time.

I follow the same engineering-grade process whether it's a CRM build, a website, or an automation workflow — because skipping steps is how systems break after handover.

01

Discover

Map your current workflow, pain points, and requirements before touching any tool. Ask the questions that prevent rework.

02

Build

Construct the system in layers — CRM first, automations second, integrations third. Clean logic with no over-engineering.

03

Test

Every trigger, every branch, every handoff — tested against real scenarios before you see the final build. No surprises on go-live.

04

Document

Full SOPs, annotated workflows, and training notes so the system runs and your team understands it — without me in the loop.
