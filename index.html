Do I need a GoHighLevel account before we start?

No. If you don't have one, I'll tell you on the call whether GHL is even the right tool for your situation. If it is, I'll walk you through getting the account and take it from there.

My GHL is half-built and half-working. Do you fix or rebuild?

Usually fix. The Systems Audit exists for exactly this — I map what's there, find where leads and follow-ups are leaking, and rank the fixes by business impact. Rebuilding only happens when the logic underneath can't be trusted, and I'll show you why before touching it.

How do you price?

Fixed scope for audits and builds, quoted in writing after the systems call once I've seen your account and workflow. Ongoing management is a flat monthly rate. You won't get an hourly bill that grows.

What time zone are you in, and will we overlap?

I'm in the Philippines (GMT+8) and schedule calls around your calendar — US, UK, and Australian clients all work. Day-to-day, you get written progress updates so nothing waits on a meeting.

What happens after handover? Do you keep access?

You get SOPs, annotated workflows, and a recorded walkthrough so your team can run the system without me. Access stays with you — I'm removed or downgraded at handover unless you choose ongoing management. Every build is designed so nothing depends on me staying in the loop.

What do I actually get on the free call?

20 minutes on your current lead flow — where leads come from, where they stall, what's manual. You leave with a written next-step plan: what to fix first, what it involves, and whether I'm the right person for it. No slides, no pressure.
