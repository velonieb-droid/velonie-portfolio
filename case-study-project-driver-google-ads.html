Case Study / Google Ads / Marketing Analytics

Project Driver: researching the next Google Ads campaign while checking the current lead-tracking setup.

Project Driver offers several services to businesses across the United States. My assignment was to investigate paid-search opportunities, review the existing local campaign and identify whether the website and CRM could accurately track a new lead through to booking.

Client: Project DriverRole: Campaign research and conversion QAPeriod: November 2026Tools: Google Ads, Keyword Planner, GA4, GHL
Status: Research and proposal preparation. An existing local Google Search campaign was reviewed. The proposed national campaign was not launched as part of this work. No live advertising settings, conversion actions or CRM automations were changed during this review.
01 / The assignment

Work out where to advertise, then check if results can be measured.

The business was considering campaigns for different service lines and markets. Before spending more, the team needed a grounded view of search demand, competition, likely click costs and the actual booking path.

Search-demand research

Compared keyword opportunities and forecasts across five South Florida service categories and national searches for Maya AI Receptionist, PPC Management and CRM Automation.

Existing campaign review

Examined a live local Search campaign, Auction Insights, impression-share losses, ad relevance, Quality Scores and landing-page destinations.

Conversion and website QA

Reviewed booking-event discrepancies, GA4 and Ads reporting differences, landing-page experience and mobile usability before proposing additional spend.

02 / How the research was structured

From keyword forecast to a potential booked consultation.

Service offer→ Keyword demand→ Competition and CPC→ Ad and landing page→ Booking event→ CRM qualification

A reported conversion is not automatically a qualified lead or a booked client. I kept Google Ads conversions, GA4 key events and CRM outcomes separate during the review.

03 / Findings and implications

Problems to resolve before increasing the budget.

Market and geography

Local targeting versus virtual delivery

The current campaign focused on Fort Lauderdale. Because several Project Driver services can be delivered remotely, I compared local and national search opportunities rather than assuming the existing geographic boundary was the best fit for every offer.

Tracking and attribution

Reported conversions need validation

Google Ads and GA4 reported different interaction and event measures. Booking-event discrepancies remained unresolved. The next step is to validate the event definitions and trace a test booking through the website, analytics and CRM before using conversion counts to optimize a new campaign.

Ad experience

Landing-page and mobile issues

The audit surfaced below-average landing-page experience indicators and mobile performance concerns. Those findings matter because paid traffic still needs a usable page and a reliable appointment path after the ad click.

Offer selection

Conditional Maya AI Receptionist pilot

Based on the research, the national Maya AI Receptionist offer became the leading candidate for a small controlled test. That was a recommendation, not a published campaign, and it remained subject to margin, budget, capacity and owner approval.

04 / Work completed and pending

A proposal based on evidence, not assumed returns.

  • Local and national keyword research and forecast comparisons.
  • Auction Insights, Quality Score and campaign review.
  • Ad-to-landing-page and booking-path QA findings.

  • Documentation of questions and risks for the campaign decision.

Not yet complete or approved

  • Launching a new campaign or claiming leads, revenue or ROAS from it.
  • Post-launch performance data, which does not yet exist for the proposal.
